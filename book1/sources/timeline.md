# Ledger: timeline

Chapter: `timeline` (Book 1, appendix C, "Timeline").
Stage: drafted, 2026-11-11 [D-005]. Compiled by a drafting agent from the verified chapters; independent check pending.

## Method

- Sources: the 25 Book 1 chapter files in `book1/chapters/`, read in `OUTLINE.md` order on 2026-11-11 (`local` re-read at the end, after its final fixes; its dates for LLaMA, Llama 2, Llama 3, 3.1, and 3.2 match the timeline). No source outside the chapters was fetched, and no event or date was added from memory.
- Extraction: every sentence containing a year was pulled from each chapter with its line number and citation, then read by hand. Each candidate event was recorded with its chapter ID, the date as the chapter gives it, and the citation the chapter attaches (same key, same page).
- Selection: 143 entries, chosen for the book's story: every event a chapter treats as a milestone in its history sections and summaries, the "History" items of each chapter's outline entry, and the main date-stamped releases of `local`, `since-2017`, and `frontier`. Left out: secondary results, benchmark scores, vendor documentation states (most "as of 2026-11" facts), and events a chapter mentions only in passing.
- Citations: each entry's citation is copied from the chapter sentence that states the event. Where an entry merges two chapter statements (for example a release date and a paper), both citations are given. Rendered author-date years can differ from the event date because the bibliography records the published version (for example `devlin2019bert` for BERT, posted October 2018); the appendix's introduction says so.
- Disputes kept unresolved, as the chapters present them: backpropagation (Linnainmaa 1970, Werbos 1974 and 1981, Parker and LeCun 1985, Rumelhart, Hinton, and Williams 1986, all from `backprop`); vanishing gradients (Hochreiter 1991 and Bengio, Simard, and Frasconi 1994, from `structure`); the start of deep learning (Schmidhuber's "at least since 1991" and the textbook's 2006, plus Schmidhuber's 2011 contest wins, from `deep-turn`); highway networks and ResNet (`depth`); emergent abilities (`scale`); Chatbot Arena's private testing (`evaluation`); the "Illusion of Thinking" dispute and the two camps on risk (`frontier`).
- Format: a Pandoc definition list (date, then one line with citation and an italic chapter link), grouped under period headings that follow the book's parts [D-008]. A list is used instead of a table because Pandoc wraps Typst tables in non-breaking figures, so a long table would overflow the page.
- Checks: `python3 tools/check.py` reports no errors for `timeline.md` (all citation keys resolve). `python3 build/build.py timeline` builds; the timeline itself runs to about 6 pages (143 entries), more than the outline's 4, followed by its reference list.

## Date discrepancies found

Events dated differently in two chapters, or in a chapter and its ledger or the bibliography. None changes a year in the timeline; the timeline follows the chapters as they stand.

1. **Kalai et al., letter counting.** `book1/chapters/prompting.md:138` says "In May 2025, Adam Tauman Kalai and colleagues asked" the DEEPSEEK question. The paper (`kalai2025why`) is arXiv v1 of 4 Sep 2025 (`book1/sources/prompting.md:14`); May 2025 is when the models were accessed (ledger PR43, `book1/sources/prompting.md:382`). `generation`, `evaluation`, and `frontier` date the same paper 2025. Suggested fix: "In a 2025 paper, Kalai and colleagues reported asking, in May 2025, ...". Not in the timeline.
2. **Mistral 7B.** `book1/chapters/local.md:41` dates it September 2023 but cites only `jiang2023mistral`, whose arXiv v1 is 10 Oct 2023 (`book1/sources/local.md:17`). The September date comes from the announcement `mistral2023mistral7b` (27 Sep 2023, `book1/sources/local.md:69`), which the chapter does not cite there. Suggested fix: add `[@mistral2023mistral7b]` to that sentence. The timeline uses the chapter's citation.
3. **Mixtral 8x7B.** `local.md:41` says December 2023 (announcement, `mistral2023mixtral`); `since-2017.md:111` (table) and `since-2017.md:143` say January 2024 (the paper, `jiang2024mixtral`); `since-2017.md:99` gives both. Not wrong, since each date is labelled by what it dates in at least one place, but `since-2017.md:143` reads as a release date. Suggested fix: "Mixtral 8x7B (released December 2023, paper January 2024)" there, or "(paper January 2024)". The timeline lists both dates.
4. **Llama 3.** `local.md:41` dates Llama 3 to April 2024 (8B and 70B) and 3.1 to July 2024; `since-2017.md:112` lists "Llama 3 (released as 3.1) | July 2024" and `since-2017.md:9` speaks of "Meta's Llama 3 paper of July 2024". Consistent if read as paper versus first release, but a reader can take the table row as Llama 3's release date. Suggested fix: label the row "Llama 3.1" or "Llama 3 (paper, with 3.1)". The timeline lists April 2024 and July 2024 separately.
5. **Date stamp in `frontier`.** `book1/chapters/frontier.md:61` says "As of 2025-11 the question ... is open"; the book's date, used everywhere else in that chapter, is 2026-11. Probably a typo. Suggested fix: "As of 2026-11".
6. **Adam.** `OUTLINE.md:102` (the `toolkit` history line) says "Adam (2014)"; `book1/chapters/toolkit.md:65` says "published at the ICLR conference in 2015", which the timeline follows. The outline marks its dates as unverified. Suggested fix: "Adam (2015)" in the outline, or "(2014 preprint, 2015 paper)" if the ledger supports the preprint date.
7. **Bibliography years that differ from the key and the chapter.** `bengio2000neural` has `year = {2001}` (`references.bib:1380`; NIPS 2000, 2001 proceedings), so `embeddings.md:63` "a conference paper of 2000" renders as "(Bengio, Ducharme, and Vincent 2001)". `abadi2015tensorflow` has `year = {2016}` (`references.bib:1140`; white paper of 9 Nov 2015, arXiv v2 2016), so `toolkit.md:113` "November 2015" renders next to "(Abadi et al. 2016)". Both are explained in the bib notes; flagged only because the rendered year contradicts the date beside it. Possible fix: none needed, or a word in the chapter ("the 2000 conference paper, published 2001").
8. **ELMo, minor.** `pretraining.md:27` says "In early 2018"; the ledger records an earlier OpenReview version of 27 Oct 2017 and arXiv of 15 Feb 2018 (`book1/sources/pretraining.md:21`, `:352`). The chapter's date matches the arXiv and NAACL versions; noted only because ELMo first appeared in 2017.
